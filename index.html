Hi, I'm Alicia!

gameplay programmer & programming lead

A passionate Computer Engineering and Computer Science major at USC, minoring in Video Game Programming.

Games pull at me from two directions at once, the technical side and the production side, and I've never really wanted to pick one. The games that stay with me are the ones that make you feel something and have a lasting impact on players. Some of my favorites are Subnautica and Detroit: Become Human for their captivating narrative, and Face of Another and Celeste for their unique mechanics.

Outside of games I love engineering. Making things, having ideas, figuring out how something should actually function. Given my major, I've worked a lot on integrating hardware and software together to make some cool projects! My favorite project so far has been recreating an old Mario game written fully in Verilog code and running on an FPGA board.

Another big part of my drive is leadership, which is most of why production interests me as much as programming does. Right now I get to do both: I'm VP of Technology at Open Alpha, USC's game dev club, and the programming lead at an indie game startup.

I love what I do, and I'm always looking for more opportunities to grow, as a developer and as a person.
